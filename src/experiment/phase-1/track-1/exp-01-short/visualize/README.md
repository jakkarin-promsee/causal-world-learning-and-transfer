# Exp 01 short visualizations

Run `python experiment/phase-1/track-1/exp-01-short/visualize/plot.py` from `src/` to regenerate these plots from the saved raw run, without rerunning the oracle. Pass `--run PATH` to use another complete 10-world Exp 01 short artifact.

`raw/ep-1.png` through `raw/ep-10.png` follow the saved sampling order. Each file shows Stage A, B, and C for one hidden world on a linear action-step axis from 0 to 18: the Stage-A maximum of 16 plus two steps of margin. The display clips the longer B and C trajectories at step 18; their raw records retain the full budgets of 144 and 1,296. The y axis is the unnormalized remaining uncertainty `U_t = log2|C_t|` in bits. Solid segments end at the last executed action; dashed suffixes are certified constant values after the runner stopped. A terminal-state box lists the last executed action step, final `U_T`, and raw final survivor count `|C_T|` for each stage.

[`summarize.png`](summarize.png) shows all ten world trajectories as thin lines and the stage mean as a thick line. This makes unusual worlds visible without treating action steps as independent samples. [`summarize-mean-sd.png`](summarize-mean-sd.png) shows the same stage means with bands of one sample standard deviation (`ddof=1`) across the ten worlds. The bands describe between-world spread, not confidence intervals for the mean. Both summaries include certified inferred suffixes and dash each mean after the last world in that stage executed an action. Their terminal boxes report each stage's mean final `U_T` and mean raw survivor count `|C_T|` across ten worlds, plus the latest actual stop step among those worlds. Mean `U_T` and mean `|C_T|` are averaged separately, so one is not the logarithm of the other. These are exploratory views of the short sample.

[`stop-step-histogram.png`](stop-step-histogram.png) counts the ten paired hidden worlds by the last action step actually executed in each stage. Bars are grouped by stage; hatched portions mean the runner certified observational equivalence with more than one survivor, while solid portions mean it reached a singleton. Numbers above bars give episode counts. A budget-exhausted episode would use crosshatching, but none occurred in this short run. The chart describes stopping time under the full-candidate greedy policy, not the stage's maximum action budget.
